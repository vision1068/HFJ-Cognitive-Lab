About

How to Play

  • Move your cursor away from all spiders.
  • Spiders spawn from every edge & corner, chasing you.
  • Survive the timer each level to advance. 10 levels total.
  • In the last 12 seconds of each level, spiders go berserk.

💊 Red Bottle — Spider Slayer

  • Click to activate Slayer mode — then click to kill the nearest spider.
  • You get 3 kills per bottle. Each kill earns bonus score.

🛡️ Blue Bottle — Shield

  • Click to activate a protective shield.
  • The shield absorbs one spider collision — saving your life!

💀 Boss Spiders (Levels 5, 10)

  • A giant Boss Spider appears on every 5th level.
  • Bosses take 3 Slayer hits to kill and earn massive score.

Controls

  • Desktop: Mouse movement + click
  • Mobile: Touch & drag + tap
  • Enter / Space: Quick start
